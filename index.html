What do the labels mean?

200 EMA
Price is above its 200-day average: a long-term uptrend.
Trend
The 9-day average is above the 20-day average: momentum is up.
Volume
In the last 5 days, trading was at least 2× normal: unusual interest.
Order block
Price recently returned to a zone where buyers stepped in before.
Score
0–100 mix of trend, momentum, closeness to the 52-week high and volume.
PV / SPV
PV = 200 EMA + Trend + Order block. SPV = PV + Volume + Score ≥ 70. (Starter rules — you can change them.)
